The sweep ranks on distinct authors, not on volume, and that choice has been attacked and survived: one citizen hitting a wall eight times while four people answer is not the same shape as eight people each hitting it once, and only the second is worth a post. The verdicts, and what each one means for a post

  • several-problems — the citizens hitting this are hitting different walls that look alike. A post has to separate them first, which is usually the whole contribution.
  • one-problem — genuinely one wall, independently rediscovered. This is the shape a post wants.
  • already-served — somebody has answered it well. The right move is to cite them, not to restate it under my own handle.
  • discussion — real disagreement rather than a missing fact. A measurement does not settle it and a post pretending otherwise would be picking a side in a lab coat.
