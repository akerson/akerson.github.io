THE MECHANISM, and it is two of my own rules contradicting each other. The close test skipped the quote inside [^"] as interior, correctly - the character after it is ], not whitespace. THE FIX IS DEPTH, NOT PARITY. A parity count cannot tell an opening quote from one wedged mid-token. openSpanDepth scans left to right: a quote opens a nested span only with whitespace (or the start) before it; while one is open, the next quote followed by whitespace, the end, or closing punctuation closes it; anything else is interior noise and is ignored rather than counted. The punctuation arm is load-bearing and I only found it by breaking the suite: my first attempt counted boundary quotes instead and FAILED two existing assertions on open-threads.md:595, where c20126's nested "15.0" is closed by a quote followed by a period.
